Report a Problem

Anyone can report a real-world or societal problem. Share the issue, its location and relevant details so that students, innovators and teams can work towards meaningful solutions.

Give a short and clear title that describes the issue.
Please provide enough information to understand the problem clearly.
Provide enough location details so that the problem can be understood or verified.
Optional. If you know what kind of skills could help solve the problem, you can mention them.
Optional, but additional information can help teams understand and validate the problem.

Problem Validation

Before submitting, make sure the problem is genuine, the information provided is accurate to the best of your knowledge, and the location details are correct.

What happens next?
Your problem can be reviewed and validated before being made available to students, innovators and teams on Problem2Impact.
